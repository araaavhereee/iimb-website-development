HUMAN INQUIRY // JAIPUR // 2026

I am a psychology student interested in understanding human behaviour and mental wellness beyond academic theory. My interests extend to communication, active listening, public speaking, creative thinking and the ways people interact with their environments.

Rather than viewing psychology as isolated clinical abstractions, I look at how cognitive models translate into ordinary human conversations, team relationships, and community spaces. What inspires someone to open up in a workshop? How do subtle environmental cues alter personal comfort? These questions drive both my academic curiosity and practical engagement.

My academic interests also include economics and its connection with human decision-making. Through studying both disciplines simultaneously, I explore the fertile friction where behavioural economics reveals how feelings, cognitive heuristics, and social pressures challenge purely rational models.

FIELD NOTE 01 BEHAVIOURAL ECONOMICS

Where rational decision theories intersect with irrational human vulnerability, choice architecture, and emotional heuristics.

FIELD NOTE 02 ACTIVE LISTENING

FIELD NOTE 03 COMMUNITY WELLNESS
